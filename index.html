I'm Fouad Zarour, a Flutter developer based in Algeria, working with teams across France and North Africa. I started in AI research and moved into mobile because I liked shipping things people touch every day.

I care about performance, honest code review, and interfaces that don't make people think. Currently exploring how AI features fit into everyday mobile products.
